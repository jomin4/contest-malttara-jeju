## Brand & Style
The design system reflects a balanced synergy between Jeju Island’s organic raw geography and hyper-competent agentic intelligence. The visual identity avoids clichéd tourist aesthetics, instead adopting an editorial, crisp travel-intelligence language: atmospheric, highly structured, serene, and responsive.

### Design Movement
**Refined Organic Tech (Glassmorphism & Crisp Precision):** 
- High-clarity frosted surfaces evoke maritime sea mist and coastal light.
- Sharp, data-dense interface components grounded by rich basalt stone neutrals and verdant emerald accents.
- Smooth kinetic transitions reflecting intelligent planning in real time.

## Colors
The color architecture captures the dynamic transitions of Jeju’s coastline and sun-drenched orchards, balanced by volcanic basalt tones.

- **Primary (`#0F766E` / Teal 700) & Vibrant Variant (`#0D9488` / Teal 600):** Anchors core actions, navigational highlights, active agentic execution states, and structural landmarks. Represents the deep emerald sea off Hyeopjae and Seongsan.
- **Secondary (`#F97316` / Orange 500) & Soft Accent (`#FB923C` / Orange 400):** Evokes the iconic Hallabong citrus and sunset horizons. Used strategically for itinerary highlights, live AI reasoning tags, callout micro-badges, and conversion actions.
- **Basalt Neutrals (`#0F172A` Slate 900 to `#F8FAFC` Slate 50):** The primary typography rests on deep volcanic slate (`#0F172A`), supported by mid-tier metadata text (`#64748B`). Surfaces span sterile crisp whites (`#FFFFFF`) to cooled mist slates (`#F1F5F9`).
- **Dividers & Structural Borders (`#E2E8F0`, `#CBD5E1`):** Subtle boundaries that maintain architectural rhythm without heavy optical weight.

## Typography
The system utilizes clean, modern grotesque typefaces optimized for bilingual rhythm (English numbers, code parameters, and modern Korean Hangul via matching geometric system fallbacks such as Pretendard and Noto Sans KR).

- **Display & Headlines (Plus Jakarta Sans):** Crafted with geometric warmth, wide proportions, and tight apertures to convey modernity and travel excitement.
- **Body & Metadata (Inter):** Highly legible, neutral sans-serif delivering effortless tracking over dense schedules, transit minutes, and AI calculation streams.
- **Letter Spacing:** Tighter letter-spacing is applied to larger sizes (`-0.02em`) to guarantee editorial authority, while small metric badges use slight positive tracking (`+0.02em` to `+0.03em`) for legibility in card metadata.

## Layout & Spacing
A fluid 12-column grid provides the backbone on desktop viewports (max-width `1440px`), collapsing into 8 columns on tablet devices (`768px - 1024px`) and 4 columns on mobile viewports (`<768px`).

- **Dual-Pane Workspaces:** Split-screen layout allows constant synchronization between itinerary cards (left 5 columns) and an interactive map/satellite timeline engine (right 7 columns).
- **Rhythm & Proximity:** Internal card elements maintain tight relational groupings via `space-xs` and `space-sm`, while top-level agent thought cards and itinerary day blocks use `space-lg` to `space-xl` to prevent cognitive fatigue during route review.

## Elevation & Depth
Elevation mimics maritime light passing through layers of atmosphere and volcanic stone. Rather than generic drop shadows, depth is achieved through layered translucency and directional basalt-tinted diffusion.

- **Level 1 (Subtle Basalt Float):** Cards and static controls use `0 1px 3px 0 rgba(15, 23, 42, 0.05), 0 1px 2px -1px rgba(15, 23, 42, 0.05)` accompanied by a crisp `1px` border of `rgba(226, 232, 240, 0.8)`.
- **Level 2 (Active/Hover Cards):** Interactive place cards and timeline checkpoints lift with `0 10px 15px -3px rgba(15, 23, 42, 0.07), 0 4px 6px -4px rgba(15, 23, 42, 0.04)`.
- **Level 3 (Modal & Agent Drawer):** `0 20px 25px -5px rgba(15, 23, 42, 0.1), 0 8px 10px -6px rgba(15, 23, 42, 0.04)`.
- **Glassmorphism Spec:** Floating headers, prompt bars, and floating filter docks feature `background: rgba(255, 255, 255, 0.82); backdrop-filter: blur(12px); -webkit-backdrop-filter: blur(12px); border: 1px solid rgba(255, 255, 255, 0.6)`.

## Shapes
A roundedness tier of `2` strikes a balance between professional precision and organic, pebble-like curvature reminiscent of Jeju’s coastal stones.

- **Standard Containers (`rounded-md` / 0.5rem):** Form inputs, prompt action buttons, diff highlights, and map controls.
- **Cards & Canvas Modals (`rounded-lg` / 1rem):** Primary itinerary timeline cards, route preview cards, and AI reasoning insight cards.
- **Pill Badges (`rounded-full`):** Category tags, transit type badges (e.g., Coastal Bus, Rental EV), time-stamps, and state indicators.

## Components

### 1. Natural Language Prompt Hero
- **Structure:** Floating glass container housing a multi-line input field, quick prompt recommendation pills ("3박 4일 동쪽 힐링 코스", "비 오는 날 실내 미술관 투어"), and an execution trigger button.
- **Styling:** Frosted white ground (`rgba(255,255,255,0.85)`), hairline slate border, emerald pulsing focus ring, and a Hallabong orange accent submit button.

### 2. AI Reasoning Flow State Cards
- **Structure:** Expandable collapsible panels displaying real-time agentic actions (e.g., "Analyzing Seongsan weather patterns", "Optimizing drive time via 1100 Road").
- **Styling:** Muted slate surface (`#F8FAFC`), emerald micro-spinners, monospace-style time indicators, and animated progress connectors.

### 3. Place Discovery & Timeline Cards
- **Structure:** Visual card containing high-fidelity photo preview, tag tray (e.g., `#OceanView`, `#Cafe`, `#PetFriendly`), drive time duration pill, and dynamic crowd density indicators.
- **Visuals:** Base white background, subtle border, Hallabong orange highlight on rating badges, and clear drag-and-drop handles for manual reordering.

### 4. Re-Planning Diff Comparison
- **Structure:** Split or before-after inline view demonstrating automated route optimization (e.g., avoiding sudden rain showers or road closures).
- **Styling:** Removed items styled with light red tint (`#FEF2F2`) and strikethrough; added/recommended items illuminated with soft emerald highlight (`#F0FDFA`) and an AI Sparkle tag.

### 5. Final Itinerary Summary
- **Structure:** Sticky or downloadable master view summarizing estimated total driving distance, carbon offset, peak budget breakdown, and visual transit graph.
- **Styling:** Basalt dark footer mode (`#0F172A`) or high-contrast clean white surface with clear data visualization dials.
